## How to use this kit

Everything you need to set up the StormStudio store on Gumroad is here: your
profile text, six products with paste-ready titles and descriptions, the price
tiers, which files to upload to each, and which cover image to use. When you say
go, we upload it all. Nothing here needs rewriting; copy and paste.

> [!key] Order of operations on Gumroad: set the Profile first, then create products top to bottom, upload the matching cover and files, set the versions/prices, and turn each one from Unpublished to Published last.

## Files you will upload (from the package)

English PDFs live in `product/pdf/`, Dutch in `product-nl/pdf/`. Covers are in
`gumroad/covers/`. Each buyer gets both languages in the download, so the product
serves the Dutch home market and the international market at once.
