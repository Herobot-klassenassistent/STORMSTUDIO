## A quick hello

Hi, I'm Kevin. I came into teaching sideways, through music. I still tour with
bands, and somewhere along the way I fell hard for the classroom and never left.
For the last eleven years I've made creative, hands-on lessons for schools all
over the Netherlands, and I'm at my happiest with the kids who don't quite fit the
standard mould.

I wrote this course because the gamification training I gave for international
teachers in Amsterdam kept getting asked for long after it was over. So I rebuilt
it, sharpened it, and put everything I actually do into one box you can pick up
and run. It's tested in real classrooms, not a slide deck about theory.

## What's in the box

Everything you need to run the course: the facilitator manual, the slide deck, a
participant workbook with assignments, step-by-step tutorials, a source list, and
this guide. Give it live, give it online, or just work through it yourself. It grew
out of the two-day course I delivered at Mediacollege Amsterdam (the Erasmus+
"Digi-Eng" project), rebuilt and expanded for 2026.

> [!key] One course in a box. No extra licences needed to run it, beyond the free or low-cost classroom tools it teaches.

## Who this course is for

- **Schools and school boards** running a study day (studiedag) or a year-long
 professionalisering track for their team.
- **Course providers** who want a proven, ready-made course to deliver.
- **Cultuureducatie and PD organisations** (CmK coordinators, arts-education hubs,
 library networks) that book trainers and want reusable material.
- **Individual teachers and trainers** who want to run it themselves or upskill.

## How you can run it

The course is deliberately modular. The full two-day course is the heart of it
(Components 00 to 04), and each of the three modules also stands on its own as a
half-day or a single session.

**The full course.** Gamification in Education, the complete two-day programme.
Deliver it live, as four online half-days, or self-paced.

**The three modules**, each self-contained with its own slides, workbook pages,
tutorials and assignments:

- **AI in Education**, practical AI for teachers: designing, differentiating and
 producing classroom material fast, plus AI literacy for students.
- **Game Design with Delightex**, a full immersive-creation track built around
 **Delightex** (formerly CoSpaces Edu): students build 3D worlds and games, code
 them with CoBlocks, and step into them in AR/VR. Includes tutorials and links to
 the Delightex approach, and ready-to-run lesson designs around the platform.
- **Inclusive Gamification**, designing game-based learning that works for
 special-needs and mixed-ability groups.

## What teachers walk away with

- **A finished, usable thing.** Every participant builds one real, playable
 gamified lesson of their own. That is the point of the whole course.
- **A way of working**, not just a technique: design a lesson fast with a little
 AI, bring it alive with game design, and make it work for every learner.
- **The inclusion method**, so game-based learning reaches the students who are
 usually left out, not just the keen ones.
- **Something tested.** This was delivered to international teacher groups and
 rated highly before it ever became a download.

## What you need to run it

- The materials in this package (included).
