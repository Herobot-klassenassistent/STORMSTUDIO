Who made it

Built by a teacher who has taught every kind of group

I'm Kevin Storm. I have been a teacher for more than twenty years, most of it in special education, working with the children who learn in every kind of way and the ones who need a very different door into a lesson. Music came later, a second career I still tour with, but the classroom has always been the constant.

This course began as a training I gave for international teachers in Amsterdam, through Erasmus+. It kept getting asked for long after it was over, so I rebuilt it for 2026 with AI and immersive learning, and put everything I actually do into one box you can pick up and run. It is tested in real classrooms, not a slide deck about theory.
