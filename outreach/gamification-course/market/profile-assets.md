## A 60 to 90 second reel script (record on the road)

Film it on your phone, one take, somewhere with good light. No script on screen, just
say it in your own words. Add captions afterwards, most people watch on mute.

> Open, looking at the camera:
> Most gamification stops at points and badges. And honestly, kids see through that in
> about a week.
>
> Then, a little slower:
> What actually pulls a class in is quieter. A clear goal. Fast feedback. A challenge
> that is the right size. A bit of choice. And a reason to care that is bigger than the
> reward.
>
> Warmer, this is the heart of it:
> I have spent twenty years teaching, a lot of it in special education, and the real
> test is always the same. Does the lesson work for the kid who usually checks out? If
> it works for them, it works for everyone.
>
> Close, simple call to action:
> I put all of it into a course for teachers, with a little AI to build lessons fast
> and some game design to bring them alive. There is a free one page guide to start
> with, the link is in the caption. Come and build something your class will not want
> to leave.
