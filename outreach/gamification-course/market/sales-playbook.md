## My verdict as your sales manager

You asked me to email more international schools. I did widen the provider net
(five Erasmus+ providers drafted now), but I want to be honest with you: cold
emails to individual schools abroad are the slowest, lowest-yield thing we can do,
and most serious international buyers hide behind web forms and proposal portals,
not public inboxes. So we run a small amount of that, and we put our real weight
behind three motions that actually sell:

1. **Provider partnerships** (Erasmus+ aggregators + Delightex). They already have
 the teachers and the budget. One yes here is worth a hundred cold school emails.
2. **Speaking / workshops** at conferences and school-association PD. You stand in
 front of a room of buyers and sell the whole room at once. This is the fastest
 way to bookings and credibility.
3. **Direct-to-decision-maker on LinkedIn.** Heads of PD, edtech leads, Erasmus
 coordinators. Warm, personal, cheap, and it fits your touring life.

Underneath all three: the **funded Dutch pipeline** (already warm, budget already
sits with the schools) is where the first euros come fastest. Do not neglect it
while we chase the world.

You told me you'll travel anywhere. Good, that is a real asset. Let's sell it:
"an international trainer who will come to you" is a strong line.

## Script 1 · Provider contact-form pitch (paste into their forms)

For iDevelop, Teachersrise, Erasmus Learning Academy, and any provider that only
has a form. Keep it short; forms reward brevity.

> Subject: Ready-made gamification course + trainer availability
>
> Hi, I'm Kevin Storm, a teacher and international teacher-trainer. I've built a
> complete, proven two-day course, Gamification & Immersive Learning (AI + game
> design with Delightex + inclusion), with outcomes mapped to Erasmus+. I'd like to
> add it to your catalogue or join you as a trainer, and I'll travel anywhere.
> Course and cv: kevinstorm.eu. Could we set up a short call?

## Script 2 · Conference / CFP pitch (call for proposals)

For edtech and teaching conferences (BETT, ISTE, national teacher congresses,
regional edtech events, Delightex/CoSpaces community events). Most run an open
"call for speakers/proposals". Submit this.

**Session title:** Build a Playable Lesson in 90 Minutes (that works for every learner)

**Abstract (paste):**
> Gamification usually stops at points and badges. In this hands-on session you'll
> build one real, playable lesson for your own classroom, using AI to design it fast,
> Delightex to make it immersive, and a simple inclusion method so no learner is left
> out. You leave with something you can teach on Monday, not a theory of engagement.
> Led by Kevin Storm, a qualified special-needs teacher and teacher-trainer with more
> than twenty years in the classroom, who has taught everything from stop-motion with
> multiple-disability learners to game design in mainstream classrooms.

**Speaker bio (short):**
> Kevin Storm is a qualified Dutch teacher with more than twenty years in the
> classroom, most of it in special education, and a curriculum developer and
> international teacher-trainer. Music is his second career. He has delivered
> gamification training for international teachers through Erasmus+ and builds courses
> that leave something behind: kevinstorm.eu.

**Where to submit:** search "[conference name] call for proposals 2026". Prioritise
BETT (London), ISTE (US), regional edtech conferences near your tour routes, and
any Delightex/CoSpaces educator event.

## Script 3 · International-school PD proposal

For international-school PD bodies and heads of school (ISS, Principals' Training
Center, regional associations like COBIS/FOBISIA/AISH, and individual international
schools). Submit via their "propose a workshop" page or email the head of PD.

> Subject: A hands-on gamification & immersive-learning workshop for your teachers
>
> Dear [name / PD team],
>
> I'm Kevin Storm, an international teacher-trainer. I offer a hands-on
> professional-development workshop, half-day to two days, where your teachers build
> one real, playable, inclusive lesson using AI, game design (Delightex, AR/VR) and a
> simple inclusion method. It's practical, not theoretical, and I tailor it to your
> curriculum and your learners, including special-needs and mixed-ability groups.
> I travel worldwide. Could we find 20 minutes to see if it fits your PD calendar?
> More: kevinstorm.eu.

## The weekly cadence (this is the job)

- **Mon:** follow up every open provider/school thread that's 5+ days quiet.
- **Tue:** submit 1 conference CFP or 1 association PD proposal.
- **Wed:** 10 LinkedIn DMs (mix of the three scripts).
- **Thu:** 1 warm Dutch study-day pitch from the existing pipeline.
- **Fri:** post one short "onderwijs vanuit de tourbus" piece; log replies.

Ten touches a week, every week, is what builds a pipeline. Consistency beats
cleverness here.

## Track it (simple pipeline)

Keep one line per lead: name, channel, date contacted, status (contacted / replied
/ call booked / proposal sent / won / lost), next action, next date. I've started
this in `international-leads.csv`; add the LinkedIn and conference rows as you go.
