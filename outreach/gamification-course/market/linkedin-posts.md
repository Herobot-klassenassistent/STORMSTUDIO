## English (international)

It is live, and I am a little nervous typing that.

I have been a teacher for more than twenty years, most of it in special education,
working with the children who learn in every kind of way and the ones who need a
very different door into a lesson. I trained as a teacher and it has been my life's
work. Music came later, a second career I still tour with, but the classroom has
always been the constant. Over the years I have designed a lot of curriculum and
taught just about every kind of group there is, and lately I poured all of that into
one thing, a full course for teachers on gamification and immersive learning, which
today I am finally opening up.

It is about designing lessons that genuinely pull a room in. You use a little AI to
build them quickly, some game design to make them come alive, and a simple way of
working so that no learner gets left behind. Everyone who does it walks away with a
real, playable lesson they can teach the very next week.

There is a free one page guide to start with, no strings, and the full course sits
behind it for anyone who wants to go further. I built it to travel too, so if you
would like it brought to your school or your team, anywhere in the world, just say
the word.

If you work in education, I would love to connect. Tell me what you are working on,
and let us see what we can make together.

Kevin
