# Ambassador and affiliate kit: get inside the tools' audiences, and let others sell

Three ways to reach teachers without cold email: become a recognised voice in an
AI-tool's community, offer that community a free workshop, and let edu creators sell
the course for a commission.

---

## 2. Free-workshop pitch to an AI tool's community (MagicSchool, Curipod, Diffit, SchoolAI, Eduaide)

Send to their community or partnerships contact (look on their site for a community,
ambassador or partnerships page, or ask in their educator community). This is warm: you
are offering their teachers something for free, not asking them to buy.

> Subject: A free workshop idea for your community
>
> Hi [name], I train teachers in gamified and inclusive lessons, and I would love to
> give your community something useful for free: a live workshop where teachers build
> one real, playable lesson using a little AI, with a strong inclusion angle for
> special-needs and mixed-ability groups. No product pitch, just a genuinely helpful
> session for your educators, and a natural fit with [tool]. I have twenty years in
> special education and I run this internationally. If it is interesting, tell me how
> you like to run these and I will make it easy. Either way, I am a fan of what you are
> building. Warm regards, Kevin Storm, kevinstorm.eu

## 3. Gumroad affiliates: let edu creators sell it for you

Gumroad has a built-in affiliate system. You only pay on a sale, so it is risk-free.

**Set it up (once):**
1. In Gumroad, go to your product, then the Affiliates area (or Settings, Affiliates).
2. Turn affiliates on and set a commission, 30 to 40 percent works and is motivating.
3. You can add specific people by email (they get a unique link), or enable open
   sign-up so any approved creator can request a link.
4. Do this on the Complete Course and the Bundle first; those are worth the most per sale.

**Who to give links to (warm, people with teacher audiences):**
- Edu-YouTubers and TikTokers who make lesson-idea or edtech content.
- Teacher-newsletter authors and edu-bloggers.
- LinkedIn education voices and course creators in the gamification / AI space.
- Delightex, CoSpaces and VR-in-education community figures.
Find them by searching those platforms for "gamification classroom", "AI for teachers",
"game based learning". Pick people whose audience is teachers, not other marketers.

**The seeding note (send with a free copy):**
> Hi [name], I really like what you share with teachers. I built a course on
> gamification, AI and inclusive lessons, and I would love to just give you a free
> copy, no strings. If you find it useful and think your audience would too, I have an
> affiliate link so you earn on anything that comes through you. No pressure at all, I
> would rather you have it and enjoy it. Here is the free copy: [link]. And if you want
> the affiliate link, just say the word. Warm regards, Kevin
