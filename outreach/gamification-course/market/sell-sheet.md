## The promise

Teachers stop hearing about gamification and start building it. In this training
every teacher leaves with one real, playable lesson of their own, ready to teach
the next week, and it works for every learner, including the most demanding.

## What teachers do

They take one of their own lessons and rebuild it: they design it fast with a
little AI, bring it alive with game design (Delightex, AR and VR), and make it
inclusive with a simple method so nobody is left out. They leave with the finished
lesson, a reusable way of working, and the confidence to keep going.

## Why this one

- **Proven.** Born as a course for international teachers in Amsterdam through
 Erasmus+, rebuilt for 2026.
- **Practical.** Everyone makes something real. No coding or gaming background needed.
- **Inclusive.** A genuine specialism: game-based learning that works for
 special-needs and mixed-ability groups, not just the keen kids.
- **Led by someone who has done it.** Kevin Storm has over twenty years designing
 curriculum and teaching hands-on to every kind of group, from stop-motion with
 multiple-disability learners to game design in mainstream secondary schools, and
 international teacher training through Erasmus+.

## How it is delivered

- **Live**, two days, at your school or event, anywhere in the world.
- **Online**, four half-days, for teams on different sites.
- **Self-paced**, a complete download teachers can run themselves.
- Also as **standalone half-day modules**: AI in Education, Game Design with
 Delightex, or Inclusive Gamification.
