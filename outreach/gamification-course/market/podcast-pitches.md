# Podcast guest pitches (ready to send)

Five warm, show-specific guest pitches. The angle is always story first, never a
product pitch: the primary teacher who spent his first decade in special education,
then toured with bands and went self-employed to keep both worlds, and now trains
teachers in gamification, AI and inclusive learning. The course is never mentioned
unless the host asks.

Kevin sends these himself. Two things to do before sending each one:
1. Verify the host name and the current way they take guest pitches (a form, an
   email, a booking link). Host line-ups change, so check the show's site or its
   "be a guest" page and adjust the greeting. Where a name is uncertain I left a
   bracket.
2. Most of these shows are English-language, so these are in English to match.

Writing rules still apply: warm, human, no em dashes, no smart quotes, clean links.

Subject: A guest idea, the touring musician who taught special needs for twenty years

I trained as a primary teacher and spent my first ten years in special education,
with the kids who do not get it on the first try. Then my music took off, I started
touring with bands, and I went self-employed so I could keep teaching and keep

learning, and my specialism is making a lesson work for the learner who usually
checks out.

I can talk very practically about building playable, inclusive lessons, where AI
genuinely helps and where it does not, and I have plenty of real stories from both
the classroom and the road. Happy to come on whenever suits you. Either way, thank
you for making the show.

## After a yes
When a host says yes, prep three or four real stories (one from the road, one from a
special-education classroom, one AI moment that landed, one that flopped) and one
genuinely useful takeaway the audience can use the next morning. Let the host steer
toward the course; do not bring it up first. If asked where people can find more,
point to kevinstorm.eu and the free guide, not a hard sell.
