## Why this matters

A conference stage puts you in front of a room of buyers at once, and it turns you
from "someone who emails" into "someone schools saw present". This is the fastest
route to credibility and bookings. Below is one strong session, ready to paste,
plus which events are open now and how to pitch each.

Lead everywhere with what makes you different: over twenty years designing
curriculum and teaching hands-on to every kind of group, from mainstream classes
to very high-demand and special-needs learners. Almost no one on an edtech stage
can say that honestly. You can.

## The session (paste this)

**Title:** Build a Playable Lesson in 90 Minutes (that works for every learner)

**Format:** hands-on workshop (90 minutes preferred; adaptable to 45 or 60).
Also offered as a talk or a panel contribution.

**Audience:** primary, secondary and vocational teachers; PD leads; anyone curious
about gamification, AI or immersive learning. No coding or gaming background needed.

**Abstract:**
> Gamification usually stops at points and badges. In this hands-on session you'll
> build one real, playable lesson for your own classroom, and leave with something
> you can teach on Monday, not a theory of engagement. You'll use a little AI to
> design it fast, a taste of game design (Delightex, AR/VR) to bring it alive, and a
> simple inclusion method so no learner is left out, including the most demanding.
> Led by Kevin Storm, who has spent more than twenty years designing curriculum and
> teaching every kind of group, from stop-motion with multiple-disability students
> to game design in mainstream secondary schools.

**What participants leave with:**
> A finished, playable lesson of their own; a reusable AI prompt for designing more;
> a six-point method for building engagement; and an inclusion checklist that makes
> any game-based lesson work for mixed-ability and special-needs groups.

**Why it lands:** it's practical, everyone makes something, and the inclusion angle
is one very few sessions offer.

## How to pitch each event

- **Bett UK:** lead with the hands-on format and the AI + inclusion combination;
 Bett loves practical, take-home sessions. Offer the 45 and 90 minute versions.
- **FETC / ISTE:** emphasise the AI and Delightex/AR-VR angle and the classroom
 project; these US audiences want tools they can use Monday.
- **Virtual events:** stress that you deliver it just as well online, and that it's
 built for teachers to build live during the session.
- **Regional / national:** add a line that you tailor to their curriculum and that
 you can deliver in English or Dutch.

## After you're accepted

- Put "As seen at [event]" on the landing page and your LinkedIn. That single line
 sells the download and the live bookings.
- Bring the free 6 Levers one-pager as a handout with the store link on it.
- Collect names in the room; that's your warmest email list.
