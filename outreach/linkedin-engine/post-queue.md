Three years ago I travelled to Oradea in Romania to teach a course on gamification to
a room full of teachers. We made an e-book together, we laughed a lot, and I came home

This week, out of nowhere, I wrote to them just to say I still think about it. They

## R3 - Building the AI class assistant
Pillar 3 + 2 · Language: English (Dutch version worth making later) · Photo: a clean
screenshot or sketch of the tool, or your laptop mid-build backstage. · First comment:
kevinstorm.eu

I am building a small AI assistant for teachers, and I keep throwing away the clever
version.

Every time I add a feature that looks impressive in a demo, I take it back out,
because a tired teacher at nine at night does not need impressive. They need the
worksheet differentiated three ways in two minutes, they need it safe with their
pupils' data, and they need it to get out of the way.

So the rule I am building to is simple. If it does not give a teacher an evening back
or help them reach the kid who was about to be left behind, it does not go in. That is
a harder product to build than a flashy one. It is the only one worth building.

If you teach: what is the one task you would hand to an assistant tonight if you
actually trusted it?

Hashtags: #buildinpublic #AIineducation #edtech #onderwijs #teaching

## R4 - Two rooms, one craft (the bridge post)
Pillar 1 + 4 · Language: English · Photo: ideally a diptych, a stage shot beside a
classroom or workshop shot. If you only have one, use the stage shot. · First comment:
kevinstorm.eu

Tonight I will stand in front of a room that paid to be there and I will have about
ten seconds to make them lean in.

Next week I will stand in front of a class that had no choice about being there, and I
will have about the same ten seconds.

People think teaching and performing are different jobs. They are the same job. You
set a hook, you read the room, you give people something to do with their hands and
their heads, and you make very sure that the person in the back row does not feel
invisible. The stage taught me how to hold attention. The classroom taught me what to
do with it once I had it.

Twenty years in, I am still learning from both. If you teach, I promise your instincts
on a stage and in a classroom are more alike than you think.

What pulls you in, as a learner, every single time?

Hashtags: #teaching #onderwijs #engagement #gamification #education
