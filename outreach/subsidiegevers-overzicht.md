# Subsidisers in the Dutch school system (extensive map)

For Kevin Storm. Goal: everyone who puts money into the kind of work you do
(curriculum, leerlijnen, teamtrainings, cultuureducatie, nieuwe media/AI/kunst,
special needs) beyond the CmK coordinators you already work with.

## First: how the money actually reaches you (3 routes)
Most of these funds do NOT pay a freelancer directly. Knowing the route tells
you who to email and how to pitch.

- **Route A - Direct commissioner.** They hire/commission makers themselves:
  CmK coordinators (Compenta etc.), museums' education departments, some funds'
  own programme teams. => You pitch yourself.
- **Route B - School applies, hires you.** A school gets a grant/regeling and
  then buys in an expert. => You arm the school: "we can do this together and
  fund it via X." Name the fund in your pitch to the school.
- **Route C - Partner + co-apply.** You team up with a stichting/cultuurinstelling
  that applies to a private fund; you are the maker in the project. => You build
  relationships with those partners, then co-write.

Below, each entry is tagged [A]/[B]/[C] for the likely route.

## 1. National culture funds (Rijkscultuurfondsen)
The six government culture funds. Several run education programmes directly.

- **Fonds voor Cultuurparticipatie (FCP)** [A/C] - THE big one for education and
  participation. Runs **Cultuureducatie met Kwaliteit** (the national roof over
  Compenta and all CmK coordinators), plus **Samen Cultuurmaken** (inclusion,
  incl. people with a disability - strong fit) and **Jij maakt het mee**. Worth a
  direct relationship with their programme teams, not just the provincial arms.
- **Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie** [A/C] - design, digital culture,
  e-culture, games. Runs programmes around **digitale cultuur** and
  design-in-education. Best national fit for your AI / gamedesign / nieuwe media
  angle. They fund makers and cross-overs with education.
- **Nederlands Filmfonds** [C] + **Netwerk Filmeducatie / EYE** [A] - film and
  media education. Fits your fotografie / stopmotion / film work. Netwerk
  Filmeducatie is the place to be known.
- **Mondriaan Fonds** [C] - visual arts and heritage; mediation/education lines.
- **Fonds Podiumkunsten** [C] - performing arts (less direct, but theatre/festival
  formats).
- **Nederlands Letterenfonds** [C] - literature (least relevant, unless verhaal/
  storytelling projects).

## 3. Special-needs & vulnerable-children funders (your core niche)
These are gold for your special-needs / VSO / mytyl / cluster work. Mostly Route
C (a school/care org co-applies) but several fund creative & participation
projects for disabled or vulnerable kids directly.

- **NSGK (Nederlandse Stichting voor het Gehandicapte Kind)** [C] - projects that
  help children/young people with a disability take part. Creative & inclusive
  projects are exactly their thing. TOP special-needs target.
- **Johanna Kinderfonds** [C] - children with a physical disability; participation
  and development. Fits mytyl / lichamelijk gehandicapt work.
- **Handicap.nl** (fusie o.a. Fonds verstandelijk gehandicapten / Revalidatiefonds)
  [C] - projects for people with a disability.
- **Cornelia-Stichting** and **Phelps Stichting** [C] - children with physical /
  multiple disabilities; small, project-based. Fit mytyl/tyltyl.
- **Kinderrevalidatie Fonds Adriaanstichting (KFA)** [C] - rehab & participation
  for children.
- **FNO** (Fonds NutsOhra) [C] - health-focused; programmes for children with a
  chronic illness or disability and "Kansen voor alle kinderen". Fits zieke
  leerlingen / OZL and special needs.
- **Stichting Kinderpostzegels Nederland** [C] - vulnerable children, education,
  participation.
- **Nationaal Fonds Kinderhulp** [C] - kids growing up in poverty; participation
  (culture/sport). Fits inclusion.
- **Jeugdeducatiefonds** [B] - primary schools with children in poverty; gives
  schools budget and materials (you found this earlier).
- **Kansfonds** [C] - vulnerable people, inclusion (Catholic roots, broad).
- **Oranje Fonds** [C] - social cohesion & participation; disability/inclusion
  projects. Big and approachable.
- **Bernard van Leer Foundation** [C] - early childhood (0-8) - niche but strong
  if you do young-child work.
- **Madurodam Kinderfonds** [C] - development projects for children.
- **Skanfonds / Kinderfonds van Dusseldorp / Zonnige Jeugd** [C] - smaller
  child/youth funds.
- **Jeugdfonds Sport & Cultuur** [B] - pays the participation costs for individual
  children (per child, via an intermediary/school). Can fund kids to join your
  workshops. Municipal, easy to name in a school pitch.

## 4. Digital, tech, media & maker funders (your AI / gamedesign edge)
- **SIDN fonds** [A/C] - "internet for everyone"; funds digital media, digital
  skills and tech-for-society projects, including education. Strong fit for AI /
  digital literacy. You can often apply as maker/initiator.
- **Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie** - see section 1 (digital culture).
- **VodafoneZiggo Foundation** [C] - digital skills for vulnerable groups
  (Online Masters etc.). Fits AI-literacy / digital confidence.
- **FutureNL** [B] - foundation for digital literacy in PO/VO; free lessons and
  programmes; they contract makers and trainers.
- **Sterk Techniekonderwijs (STO)** [B] - regional VMBO technology money; creative
  tech / maker education can qualify. Approach the regional STO partnership.
- **Platform Talent voor Technologie / Techniekpact** [B] - regional tech-education
  funding and networks.
- **Google.org / corporate tech-education grants** [C] - occasional calls for
  digital-skills education.

## 5. Government regelingen that give SCHOOLS money to hire you (Route B)
These are administered by **DUS-I** (Dienst Uitvoering Subsidies aan Instellingen)
or OCW. The school applies; then it can buy in an external expert. Name the fit
fund when you pitch a school.

- **Subsidie Verbetering Basisvaardigheden** - schools get money for taal, rekenen,
  burgerschap AND **digitale geletterdheid**. Your AI/media/digital-skills training
  fits digitale geletterdheid. (CHECK current round/status - this regeling evolves.)
- **Schoolkracht** (DUS-I) - schools get funds for school development and can hire
  external expertise. Broad and useful.
- **Regeling Cultuureducatie / CmK** - the school-facing side of CmK.
- **Sterk Techniekonderwijs** - see section 4.
- **Gezonde School / Jong Leren Eten** - themed programmes (less your core, but
  media/creative angles exist).
- **Nationaal Programma Onderwijs (NPO)** - largely wound down by 2025, but some
  schools still carry menukaart-style budgets; worth asking a school if any remains.
- **Onderwijsachterstandenbeleid (OAB/GOAB)** - municipal money for schools with
  disadvantage; can fund enrichment.

## 6. Provincial & municipal routes
- **Provincial cultuurnota / cultuureducatie budgets** - every province funds
  cultuureducatie via its CmK coordinator (you've covered most) and sometimes
  directly.
- **Gemeentelijke cultuureducatie & jeugd budgets** - municipalities fund local
  culture education; ask the gemeente's cultuur/onderwijs ambtenaar.
- **Brede Regeling Combinatiefuncties (cultuurcoaches / buurtcultuurcoaches)** [A/B]
  - municipalities co-fund cultuurcoaches who connect schools with makers. Getting
  known by local cultuurcoaches = a steady booking channel.
- **Jeugdfonds Sport & Cultuur** - see section 3.

## 7. European funding
- **Erasmus+ KA1 (mobility)** [A] - you already deliver these teacher courses;
  schools get EU money to send staff. Nuffic administers KA1 for NL schools -
  worth being a known course provider.
- **Erasmus+ KA2 (partnerships)** [C] - schools/orgs co-fund innovation projects;
  you can be the expert partner.
- **Creative Europe** [C] - EU culture-sector projects (bigger, consortium-based).
- **eTwinning / European School Education Platform** [A] - online school
  collaboration; ambassador/trainer roles.

## 8. Corporate, bank & lottery foundations
Local and quick; good for concrete school projects (Route C).

- **Rabo ClubSupport / Rabobank Coöperatiefonds** [C] - local Rabobanks fund
  community & school projects. Very approachable regionally.
- **VriendenLoterij Fonds / Nationale Postcode Loterij** beneficiaries [C] - fund
  culture & social organisations that could host your work.
- **VodafoneZiggo Foundation / KPN Mooiste Contact Fonds** [C] - digital &
  connection projects.
- **ING Fonds / ABN AMRO Foundation / ASN Foundation** [C] - social & education.

## 9. Not funders, but the doors that open funding (get known here)
- **LKCA** (Landelijk Kennisinstituut Cultuureducatie en Amateurkunst) - the hub;
  has a **netwerk cultuureducatie speciaal onderwijs**. Being known here connects
  you to funders, coordinators and peers. High-value relationship.
- **Meer Muziek in de Klas** - national, funds & runs music education + teacher
  training (train-the-teacher, your model).
- **Netwerk Filmeducatie** - film/media education network.
- **Cultuurconnectie** - branch body for centres for the arts (many potential
  bookers of freelancers).
