## Snapshot

Native Dutch educator and curriculum designer with more than twenty years in the
classroom, a specialism in special-needs and creative-media education, and hands-on
experience building learning tools with AI. I evaluate and improve AI output for
quality, pedagogy, factual accuracy, tone and accessibility, and I write and reason
clearly in both Dutch and English.

## Why I am a strong subject-matter expert

- **Native Dutch (nl-NL).** Fluent, publication-level written Dutch; I write a
 monthly column. Ideal for Dutch-language data, localisation, and nl-NL evaluation.
- **Education domain depth.** Twenty years teaching and designing curriculum, from
 special education to international teacher training. I can judge whether an
 explanation actually teaches, not just whether it is correct.
- **Structured, critical feedback.** My daily work is assessing output and giving
 precise, rubric-based feedback, exactly the skill these tasks reward.
- **Special-needs and accessibility lens.** I catch where content fails the people
 who do not get it by default: clarity, reading level, tone, inclusion.
- **AI-fluent.** I design lessons and small tools with AI and teach others to do
 the same; I understand prompts, model behaviour, and where models go wrong.
- **Reliable and async.** I work independently to deadlines from anywhere (I also
 tour as a musician), and I follow guidelines carefully.

## Areas I can cover

- Dutch language: writing, editing, grammar, translation EN and NL, localisation quality.
- Education and pedagogy: lesson content, explanations, tutoring dialogues, rubrics.
- Creative and media: writing, design thinking, arts education.
- Accessibility and plain language: readability, inclusive tone, cognitive load.

## Where to apply, and what each wants

- **Mercor**: expert talent network, among the highest pay for subject-matter
 experts. Wants a strong CV and LinkedIn, plus a screening interview. Lead with
 education, Dutch and writing.
- **Outlier (Scale AI)**: broad, 30+ languages including Dutch. Sign up, complete
 the assessments, then pick projects. Strong for language and education tasks.
- **Mindrift (Toloka)**: expert-led AI projects; profile plus skills tests.
- **Alignerr (Labelbox)**: expert evaluation projects; application and screening.
- **HiredBuddy**: rolling out across Europe in 2026; register early.
- **jazzer.nl**: a Dutch portal that aggregates AI-training jobs; useful for
 nl-specific gigs.

1. Use the tailored CV and this profile text for the bio and about fields.

- Acceptance and task flow vary. Treat it as a portfolio of two or three platforms,
 not a single job.
- Pay and volume fluctuate. The language plus education combination is among the
 steadier, better-paid niches.
