# Remote work you can actually do from the tourbus (creative angles)

Companion to `organisaties.md`. That file lists *who might hire you*. This one
is about *what work formats fit your life* - things you can build daily from the
road, deliver in sprints, and ideally make once and sell many times.

Your unfair advantages, stated plainly:
- 20 years in education, 11 freelance; gediplomeerd speciaal onderwijs.
- You build the higher-level stuff: curriculum, leerlijnen, teamtrainingen.
- Nieuwe media, kunst, AI, gamedesign, stopmotion, fotografie, film.
- You train teachers internationally (Erasmus "gamification in het onderwijs").
- You are a genuinely touring musician. Almost nobody in edtech can say that.
- You work async every day and only need to be in the room for the key moments.

The strategy in one line: **turn the things you already make into products and
remote services, so income is not tied to you standing in a classroom.**

---

## 1. Productize what you ALREADY teach (make once, sell many times)
This is the biggest shift: you already have the content, you are just delivering
it live each time. Package it and it earns while you are on stage.

- **Self-paced online course: "Gamification in het onderwijs".** You already run
  this for Erasmus. Record it once (Dutch + English), sell to teachers and
  schools year-round. Host on your own site (Copecart / Plug&Pay / Teachable) so
  you keep the margin. Leave-behind + passive income + funnel to teamtrainings.
- **"AI voor docenten" course + prompt-pack.** Teachers are desperate for this.
  A short paid course plus a downloadable pack of classroom-ready AI prompts and
  mini-assignments. Update it a few times a year from the road.
- **Downloadable leerlijnen & lesson kits.** Your stopmotion, fotografie, media
  and gamedesign lessons, cleaned up as ready-to-run packs. Sell per download or
  as a bundle. Channels: your own site (Gumroad/Plug&Pay), and NL marketplaces
  like lesmateriaal platforms; Teachers Pay Teachers for the English versions.
- **Templates & toolkits.** Stopmotion starter kit, "festival in de klas" project
  template, AI-lesson canvas. Cheap, high-volume, and they advertise your bigger
  services.

## 2. Remote services you can deliver in bursts
Live but online, so no travel; recordable so each one becomes reusable content.

- **Online teamtrainingen / webinars** via Teams/Zoom for schools, SWV's, cluster
  boards (the ones we are mailing). Record with permission = reusable material.
- **Curriculum / leerlijn development as a remote deliverable.** Frame it exactly
  as async: "I develop the leerlijn from the road, deliver in sprints, and join
  online for review sessions." This is the org-doc work, sold as remote-first.
- **AI-coaching hours for teachers/teams.** 1-on-1 or small-group online sessions
  helping teachers build their own tools. Sell as a strip card (strippenkaart).
- **"Second opinion" / consult for edtech & culture orgs.** Paid advisory: they
  send you a concept, you review pedagogy and give feedback async. Retainer-able.

## 3. Build small AI tools for education (your vibe-coding edge)
You are already building things (and Praktikon/Luuk asked you about a vibe-coding
workshop - that interest is real). This is remote-native, high-value work.

- **Custom GPTs / mini-apps for schools.** A reading-buddy bot, a rubric
  generator, a stopmotion-planner, a differentiation helper for special needs.
  Build once, license per school, maintain from anywhere.
- **Vibe-coding workshops & trainings** (live-online or recorded): teach teachers
  and teams to build their own small tools with AI. Unique, in-demand, scalable.
- **Co-develop grant-funded tools.** Partner with a stichting/culture org that
  applies to SIDN fonds or Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie (digital culture);
  you are the maker, working remotely. Real budget, real leave-behind.

## 4. Your music world, turned into paid education work (nobody else has this)
This is your most distinctive angle. Lean into it, do not hide it.

- **Music + new-media crossover materials.** A songwriting-meets-AI leerlijn,
  "maak je eigen festival" project, sound-design lessons. Sell to Meer Muziek in
  de Klas, pop-education bodies, methode publishers.
- **"Onderwijs vanuit de tourbus" content channel.** LinkedIn + a simple
  newsletter/vlog: what you build and learn on the road, AI-in-education tips,
  behind-the-scenes. This is marketing that pays you back in inbound leads,
  speaking invites, and eventually sponsorship. Your touring life IS the hook.
- **Entrepreneurship / creative-career module.** "How a working musician builds a
  freelance life" - a real, honest module for VO/MBO/PABO. You are the case study.

## 5. Platforms & marketplaces to plug into (steady inbound)
Set these up once; they feed you work without cold outreach.

- **Erasmus+ course-provider subcontracting** (Europass Teacher Academy, ITC,
  Teacher Academy) - burst delivery abroad fits touring; see org-doc.
- **Freelance-onderwijs marktplaatsen:** Freelance.nl, Hoofdkraan, InhuurdeskEDU
  and onderwijs-detachering desks - list yourself as a remote curriculum/AI
  trainer so assignments come to you.
- **Course marketplaces** for reach once your course exists (Soofos and similar),
  vs. self-hosting for margin. Do both: self-host the main funnel, list a lite
  version for discovery.
- **Speaker / workshop directories** for edu conferences (many take remote
  keynotes now) - paid, high-visibility, and great funnel to trainings.

## 6. Adjacent paid gigs that are naturally remote
Low-effort ways to earn and stay visible between the big projects.

- **Jury / panel / advisory work:** grant juries (culture/education/digital),
  edu-innovation hackathons, edtech pitch panels. Usually a fee/honorarium, often
  remote. Ask LKCA, funds, and Erasmus networks to put you on their lists.
- **Freelance authoring / editing** for method publishers (Blink, Malmberg,
  Codename Future) - fully remote writing; see org-doc.
- **Localisation NL <-> EN** of educational content and courses - you work in both
  and understand the pedagogy, not just the words.
- **Beta-testing / pedagogical review for edtech startups** - paid, async, and it
  keeps you current on the tools you then teach.
